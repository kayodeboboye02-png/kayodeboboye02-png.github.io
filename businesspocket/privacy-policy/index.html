BusinessPocket Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 October 2026

1. Introduction

BusinessPocket is a business-management application designed to help small-business owners organise products, record sales and expenses, monitor stock levels, and review business activity.

This Privacy Policy explains how BusinessPocket handles information when you use the application. We aim to minimise the information collected and give users control over their business records.

2. Information You Enter

Depending on the features you use, BusinessPocket may store information such as:

  • Product names and descriptions
  • Product purchase costs and selling prices
  • Stock quantities and low-stock thresholds
  • Sales transactions and quantities sold
  • Business expenses
  • Transaction dates and related business records
  • Preferences you configure within the application

You should avoid entering unnecessary personal information about customers, employees, suppliers, or other individuals.

3. Where Your Information Is Stored

BusinessPocket is designed primarily for local use. Business records are stored on your device using the application's local storage and database. The core business-management features are intended to work without requiring an online account.

Local storage does not mean your information is automatically backed up to another device. If your phone is lost, damaged, reset, or the app's data is removed, information stored only on that device may be lost. You are responsible for keeping any available backups in a safe location.

4. Accounts and App Lock

BusinessPocket V1 does not require users to register for an online account to access its core business-management features.

The optional app lock is designed to restrict access to BusinessPocket on the device. If enabled, users can protect the application with a locally configured PIN. The app lock is separate from an online account password. It does not provide remote account recovery.

If the PIN recovery feature is enabled, users may be able to reset their PIN using a recovery code. Users should keep this code secure. The app lock does not replace the device's own security features, encryption, or physical protection.

5. AI-Assisted Features

BusinessPocket may provide optional AI-assisted explanations or business insights.

If an AI feature sends information to an external AI service, the information submitted may be processed by that service to generate a response. This processing is separate from the app's ordinary local database operations. Before using such a feature, users should be informed what information may be sent and to which service.

Users should not submit passwords, payment-card information, bank login credentials, or unnecessary personal information to an AI assistant.

Currently, AI features are powered by Groq. Only the specific business data you choose to analyze may be sent to Groq to generate insights. No data is sent without your action.

6. Backups, Exports and Sharing

BusinessPocket may provide features that allow users to export or back up business records. When users create an export or backup, the resulting file may contain business information. The file may be accessible to other apps or people if the user shares it or stores it in an insecure location.

Users are responsible for choosing secure destinations for exported files and sharing them only with intended recipients.

7. Permissions

BusinessPocket may request device permissions when needed for a feature, such as selecting or saving an exported file or sharing information through another application. The exact permissions depend on the features included in the installed version. We do not intend to request permissions unrelated to the app's functionality.

8. Data Sharing

BusinessPocket's core local business-management functionality is designed to operate without requiring users to create an online account. Information may leave the device when users deliberately use features that communicate with external services, such as AI assistance, or choose to share, export, or back up records through other applications.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Business records stored locally generally remain on the device until users delete them, remove the application's data, or uninstall the app, subject to the operating system and any available backup or export mechanisms.

Users should export or back up important records before resetting their device or removing the application. Deleting information inside BusinessPocket may not delete copies previously exported, shared, or stored elsewhere.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical measures appropriate to the application's design to help protect information. However, no application or storage method can guarantee absolute security. Users should keep their devices updated, use appropriate device security, protect their recovery codes, and avoid sharing sensitive business records unnecessarily.

11. Children's Privacy

BusinessPocket is designed for people managing business activities and is not intended to target children.

12. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when BusinessPocket's features, data practices, or legal obligations change. An updated version will be published at the privacy-policy URL provided in the application or on its official website.
